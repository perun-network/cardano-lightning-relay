## v0.3.0 - Volta

Lightning-to-Cardano bridge relay — MS3 fallback-path delivery.
Funds are locked on both sides (Lightning + Cardano), with timeout/HTLC recovery.

### Features
- Onramp swaps (Lightning BTC → Cardano cBTC): CreateInvoice/FulfillInvoice
- Offramp swaps (Cardano cBTC → Lightning BTC): CreateOfframp/FulfillOfframp
- Automatic timeout recovery: background tasks auto-cancel expired invoices and offramps
- Pool liquidity management (deposit/withdraw) via REST API and CLI
- CancelInvoice and CancelOfframp redeemers for on-chain recovery

### E2E Evidence
- 5 onramp + 5 offramp end-to-end exchanges on local devnet
- 31/31 assertions passed
- All Cardano TX hashes and Lightning payment hashes recorded
- Pool liquidity conservation verified (50M → 40M after channel balance shift)
